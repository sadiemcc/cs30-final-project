# Reflection

## What advice would you give to yourself if you were to start a project like this again?
- Have a lot less things that I want to work on
- Do the hardest things first, then make it pretty after
- Work on it whenever I have free time

## Did you complete everything in your “needs to have” list?
- Yes, but I wish I did a few on them differently

## What was the hardest part of the project?
- The collision. It was a brand new library that I tried to understand many times. I didn't get it until I was sick at home messing with it and it worked.

## Were there any problems you could not solve?
